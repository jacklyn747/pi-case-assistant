# Harlow & Reyes Injury Law (fictional): Data Handling Policy

## Why this matters
Client files hold medical records, Social Security numbers, insurance details and family information. Clients trust the firm with it. That trust is the business.

## Where client data lives
Client data stays on firm-controlled systems. It is not pasted into public AI chat tools, consumer file-sharing apps or personal email.

## The case assistant
The firm's case assistant runs on firm hardware. The language model, the search index and the audit log are all stored locally. Questions and documents are not sent to any outside AI provider.

Staff may use the assistant to find information in firm procedures and in the files of matters they are working on. Each question is logged with the user, the matter and the documents used.

## Access
Staff see only the matters they are assigned to. When a matter closes, its files move to the closed archive and are removed from the assistant's index.

## Incidents
Report any suspected exposure of client data to the managing attorney the same day.
