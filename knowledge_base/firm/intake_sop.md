# Harlow & Reyes Injury Law (fictional): New Client Intake

## Purpose
Every potential client gets the same intake, so nothing is missed in the first call and the attorney can decide on representation within one business day.

## First call checklist
Collect these before the call ends:
- Full name, best phone number and email.
- Date, time and location of the incident.
- What happened, in the caller's own words.
- Injuries and where treatment has happened so far.
- Whether police, a store manager or anyone else made a report, and the report number if they have it.
- Insurance on both sides, if known.
- Whether the caller has spoken to any insurance adjuster or signed anything.

Tell the caller not to give a recorded statement to any insurer until an attorney calls back.

## Conflict check
Run every adverse party name, insurer and witness through the conflict system before the attorney reviews the file. No substantive advice before the conflict check clears.

## Deadline screen
At intake, calculate the suit filing deadline and flag the file red if it is within 120 days. Flag immediately if a city, county or state agency may be involved, because written notice deadlines for governmental units can be as short as six months, and some city charters are shorter.

## Opening the matter
If the attorney accepts the case, open a matter number in the format M-#### within one business day, send the engagement letter and HIPAA authorization, and schedule the welcome call.
