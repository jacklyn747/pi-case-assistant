# Harlow & Reyes Injury Law (fictional): Demand Package Checklist

## Before drafting
The demand is drafted only when treatment is complete or the treating provider has given a future care estimate. Confirm all records and itemized bills are in the file.

## Contents
1. Cover letter with the claim number and a deadline for response, usually 30 days.
2. Facts of the incident and why the other party is responsible.
3. Injuries and treatment, in date order, with provider names.
4. Medical specials: a table of every provider with amounts paid or incurred, matched to the billing ledgers.
5. Lost wages, with an employer letter or pay records.
6. Non-economic damages: pain, mental anguish and impairment, supported by the client's own account and treatment notes.
7. Photos, the police or incident report, and witness statements.

## Comparative responsibility
Address the client's share of fault head-on. Under Texas proportionate responsibility, damages are reduced by the client's percentage of responsibility, and a client found more than 50 percent responsible recovers nothing. If the insurer is likely to argue fault, the demand should answer it with evidence.

## Review
The handling attorney reviews every demand before it is sent. Calendar the response deadline and a follow-up call one week before it.
