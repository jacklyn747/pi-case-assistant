# Harlow & Reyes Injury Law (fictional): Medical Records and Bills

## When to request
Send records and billing requests to every provider within five business days of receiving the signed HIPAA authorization. Request again 30 days after the client's final treatment date so the file is complete before the demand.

## What to request
Ask each provider for two things separately: the medical records and the itemized billing ledger. Billing departments and records departments are often different offices with different fax numbers.

For the billing ledger, ask for amounts charged, amounts paid, amounts adjusted or written off, and who paid. Texas limits recovery of medical expenses to amounts actually paid or incurred, so adjustments matter for the demand.

## Follow up
Follow up on every outstanding request at 14 days and 30 days. Log each follow-up in the matter's treatment log with the date and who was contacted.

## Handling
Medical records are stored only in the matter folder. Do not email records to personal addresses or upload them to outside tools. See the data handling policy.
