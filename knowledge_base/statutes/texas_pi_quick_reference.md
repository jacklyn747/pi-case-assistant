# Texas Personal Injury Quick Reference

Plain-language summaries with citations. Always confirm against the official text (run `scripts/fetch_statutes.py`) and the facts of the matter.

## Filing deadline for personal injury
A suit for personal injury generally must be filed within two years after the day the claim accrues. Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.003.

## Minors
If the injured person is under 18 when the claim accrues, the time limit generally does not run until they turn 18. Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.001.

## Deadlines that land on a weekend or holiday
If the last day falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, the deadline extends to the next day that is not. Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.072.

## Claims against governmental units
A governmental unit must receive written notice of the claim within six months of the incident. City charters may require notice sooner. Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 101.101.

## Proportionate responsibility
A claimant's damages are reduced by their own percentage of responsibility. A claimant found more than 50 percent responsible cannot recover. Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code §§ 33.001, 33.012.

## Medical expenses
Recovery of medical or health care expenses is limited to the amount actually paid or incurred by or on behalf of the claimant. Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 41.0105.
